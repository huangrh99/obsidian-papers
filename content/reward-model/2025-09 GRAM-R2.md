# GRAM-R2

## 📌 核心贡献

> 提出自训练方法 GRAM-R2，利用无标注数据激发生成式奖励模型的奖励推理能力，产出偏好标签和推理理由，可作为奖励推理的基础模型在多种任务上零/少样本适配。

## 📖 摘要

Significant progress in reward modeling over recent years has been driven by a paradigm shift from task-specific designs towards generalist reward models. Despite this trend, developing effective reward models remains a fundamental challenge: the heavy reliance on large-scale labeled preference data. Pre-training on abundant unlabeled data offers a promising direction, but existing approaches fall short of instilling explicit reasoning into reward models. To bridge this gap, we propose a self-training approach that leverages unlabeled data to elicit reward reasoning in reward models. Based on this approach, we develop GRAM-R2, a generative reward model trained to produce not only preference labels but also accompanying reward rationales. GRAM-R2 can serve as a foundation model for reward reasoning and can be applied to a wide range of tasks with minimal or no additional fine-tuning. It can support downstream applications such as response ranking and task-specific reward tuning. Experiments on response ranking, task adaptation, and reinforcement learning from human feedback demonstrate that GRAM-R2 consistently delivers strong performance, outperforming several strong discriminative and generative baselines.

## 📝 我的笔记

### 动机与问题定义

现有奖励模型的核心挑战：
1. **数据依赖**：高度依赖大规模标注偏好数据
2. **缺乏推理**：现有预训练方法无法为奖励模型注入显式推理能力
3. **泛化性**：任务特定的 RM 难以迁移到新任务

GRAM-R2 的目标是构建一个可作为基础模型的生成式奖励模型，通过自训练在无标注数据上学习奖励推理。

### 方法细节

**核心思路：** 自训练 (Self-Training)
- 利用无标注数据激发模型的奖励推理能力
- 模型不仅输出偏好标签，还生成推理理由 (reward rationales)

**关键特点：**
- 生成式奖励模型：同时输出偏好判断和推理过程
- 基础模型特性：可在多种下游任务上进行零/少样本适配
- 支持响应排序、任务特定奖励微调、RLHF 等应用

### 关键实验结果

GRAM-R2 在三个维度上展示了强劲性能：

1. **响应排序**：超越多个强判别式和生成式基线
2. **任务适配**：在少量或零额外微调下适配新任务
3. **RLHF 应用**：作为 RLHF 奖励信号时表现一致且强劲

### 总结

GRAM-R2 提出了一个有前景的方向：通过自训练在无标注数据上学习奖励推理，构建生成式奖励基础模型。被 AAAI 2026 接收，说明其方法论和实验结果获得了认可。这一方向与 GenRM 和 DeepSeek-GRM 的生成式奖励建模思路一脉相承，但更强调无标注数据的利用和推理能力的培养。
