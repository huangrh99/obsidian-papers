# CogView4

## 📌 核心贡献

> 智谱 AI 发布的高质量文本到图像扩散模型，采用单流 DiT（Diffusion Transformer）架构，支持中英双语理解与高保真图像生成。作为 GLM-Image 的扩散 decoder backbone 使用，擅长文字渲染和知识密集型视觉生成任务。

## 📖 摘要

CogView4 is ZhipuAI's text-to-image generation model based on single-stream Diffusion Transformer (DiT) architecture. It supports both Chinese and English prompts and achieves high-fidelity image generation. The model serves as the diffusion decoder backbone in GLM-Image's hybrid AR+Diffusion architecture.
